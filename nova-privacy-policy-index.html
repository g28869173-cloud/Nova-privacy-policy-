NOVA Image Studio

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Application package: com.nova.imagestudio

This Privacy Policy explains how NOVA Image Studio ("NOVA", "we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, processes, and protects information when you use the application.

1. Information We Process

NOVA is designed to minimize personal information collection. You do not need to provide your name, email address, or phone number to use the app. To operate the service, our systems may process:

  • An automatically generated device or installation identifier.
  • An anonymous authentication token or related authentication data.
  • Image prompts, selected styles, generation request identifiers, and generation status.
  • Generation-credit balances and rewarded-ad transaction or verification identifiers.
  • Technical and diagnostic information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service.

2. Prompts and AI Image Generation

When you request an image, your prompt and selected generation settings are sent to our backend and forwarded to AI Horde to process the request. AI Horde and participating generation workers may process the information needed to generate the image. Do not include confidential, sensitive, or identifying information in prompts.

AI Horde information: https://aihorde.net/

3. Generated Images and Local History

NOVA may keep up to five recent generated images in local application storage. You may also choose to save generated images to your device's photo library. Local images can generally be removed through the app or by clearing the app's data in your device settings.

Local history is distinct from information that may be temporarily processed by the backend or third-party generation services.

4. How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Provide AI image generation and return results.
  • Manage daily free generation limits and additional credits.
  • Verify rewarded-ad completions and prevent duplicate or fraudulent rewards.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
  • Detect abuse and comply with applicable legal obligations.

5. Third-Party Services

NOVA relies on third-party services. Those providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

AI Horde: Receives prompts and generation settings needed to produce requested images. See AI Horde.

Google AdMob: NOVA uses rewarded advertisements. Google and its partners may process advertising identifiers (where permitted), device information, ad interaction data, and information needed to serve or measure ads. The app's ad configuration does not by itself guarantee that no device or technical data is collected. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising information.

Supabase and backend infrastructure: Backend services may process anonymous device/session identifiers, authentication data, prompts, generation status, credit balances, and reward-verification records to operate the service. See Supabase's Privacy Policy.

6. Advertisements and Rewarded Credits

Rewarded ads are optional. After a qualifying ad completion is verified, NOVA may add the advertised number of generation credits to your balance. Server-side verification is used to help prevent duplicate or fraudulent reward claims. You can use available free credits without choosing to watch an ad.

7. Storage and Retention

Generated-image history is stored locally on your device. Backend systems may retain device/session identifiers, authentication-related data, prompts, generation records, credit records, and ad-verification records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide, secure, and troubleshoot the service, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Third-party providers may retain information under their own retention practices. Actual retention periods may vary by provider and data type.

8. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share or allow processing of information only as needed to provide image generation, advertising, reward verification, hosting and security, or to comply with legal obligations and protect the service.

9. App Permissions

NOVA may request photo or media access when you choose to save generated images. Such access is used for image-saving functionality. The core image-generation service does not require access to your contacts, microphone, or precise location.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information, which may include server-side authentication, restricted backend access, and rewarded-ad verification. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

11. Children's Privacy

NOVA is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

12. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information. You can remove locally stored images through the app or clear the app's local data in your device settings. For requests involving backend data, contact us using the details below. We may need limited information to identify the relevant records and verify a request.

13. International Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their processing is subject to their own privacy and security practices and applicable legal requirements.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its services, or applicable legal requirements change. The updated policy will be published at this URL, with a revised effective date where appropriate.
